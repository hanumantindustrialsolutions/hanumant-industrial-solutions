About Hanumant Industrial Solutions

Hanumant Industrial Solutions is based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh and is developing a focused supply network for Government, PSU, Railway and institutional procurement.

We identify procurement opportunities, study technical requirements, coordinate with manufacturers and authorised distribution channels, and manage tender, commercial and supply processes for suitable products.

Our approach centres on long-term manufacturer relationships and compliant, dependable, commercially competitive supply solutions.
